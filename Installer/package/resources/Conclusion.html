Your Rane SL 2 is Ready to Play!

The CoreAudio daemon has been restarted automatically. You can plug in your Rane SL 2 right now — no reboot is required.

Quick Tips:

  • Audio & DVS Software: Select "SL 2" in Serato DJ Pro (up to 2.5.5), VirtualDJ, Traktor, Mixxx, Ableton Live, or Logic Pro.
  • Automatic Thru Switching: Decks 1 & 2 automatically switch to USB Audio when an audio app starts streaming, and return to Analog Thru when closed.
  • SL 2 Control Panel: open it from Applications to see the SL 2's status and switch Analog Thru per deck. Phono / Line is the switch on the box.
  • Terminal CLI: Run sl2panel status, sl2panel thru, sl2panel usb, sl2panel sr 48000 or sl2panel safety 2 (the input safety offset, in ms) at any time from Terminal.
